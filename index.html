⚔️ Tower Wars

Build a winding maze of towers that forces creeps the long way down to your single exit gap — you can't fully wall it off. Earn income over time and send your recruited monsters at the enemy: every send permanently grows your income and chips their 20 lives. First base to fall loses.

You start with 320 gold to lay your maze. Recruit a bigger army on the Army screen. This match is vs AI (PvE) — real-time 1v1 vs live players is the next step.

⛏️ Mines

Buildings produce resources over time — even while you're away (up to 8h). Tap Collect, then upgrade to earn faster.
